Rebboo Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026. This policy covers the Rebboo iPhone app that splits a song into vocals, drums, bass, and other.

Who we are

The app is published by chuan gao. Contact: 261376169@qq.com.

Audio stays on your iPhone

Songs you pick, recordings you make, and the split result stay on your iPhone. Splitting runs on the phone. The app has no account and does not upload your audio to us.

Permissions

Microphone: used only when you record.

Purchases

Rebboo Pro is a monthly subscription processed by Apple. Apple handles payment. We do not receive your card number. Apple may tell the app whether the subscription is active.

Usage events

The app sends usage events, such as the app opening or a split finishing, to Aptabase in the United States. Aptabase also receives the operating system, app version, and language. These events do not include your audio, song titles, name, or a lasting device id. They are used to see which features are used. They are not used for ads or to track you across other companies’ apps.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data or show ads.

Contact

Questions about this policy: 261376169@qq.com.
