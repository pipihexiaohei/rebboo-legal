Film Camera Multi Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026. This policy covers the Film Camera Multi iPhone app, a multi-camera recorder.

Who we are

The app is published by chuan gao. Contact: 261376169@qq.com.

Everything stays on your iPhone

Videos and photos you record, and the edits you make, stay on your iPhone. The app has no account and does not upload your footage to us or to anyone else.

The app does not send analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or a device id. It has no third-party tracking software.

Permissions

Camera — used while you are recording.

Microphone — used while you are recording, to record the sound on set.

Photo library (add only) — used only when you save a finished video to your gallery. The app cannot read the photos and videos already in your library.

Purchases

The app is sold once, for a one-time price, by Apple on the App Store. There is no subscription and nothing renews. Apple handles the payment and we do not receive your name, card number, or Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether you own it.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell, or share your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: 261376169@qq.com.
