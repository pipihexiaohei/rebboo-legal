Where are my videos

Clips stay inside the app and are not in the Photos app until you save them. Tap the clip, then save it to your gallery.

Camera or microphone will not start

Open iPhone Settings, scroll to the app, and turn on Camera and Microphone. If another app is using the camera, close it and open this app again.
